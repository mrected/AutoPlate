# Project Brief: AutoPlate.net

## Overview
AutoPlate.net is the public-facing website for AutoPlate, a manufacturer of custom license plates and license plate frames for vehicles. The site serves as a product catalog and informational resource for business customers (B2B).

## Core Requirements
1. **Product Showcase**: Display all product lines with images, descriptions, material options, and pricing tables
2. **Art & Template Resources**: Provide art specification requirements and downloadable design templates for customers preparing artwork
3. **Catalog Downloads**: Offer downloadable PDF product catalogs
4. **Contact & Supplier Info**: Display ASI/SAGE/UPIC supplier identification numbers
5. **Privacy Policy**: Display company privacy policy

## Product Lines
- **Screen Printed Plates** — Available in .075", .055", .035", .023" Hi-Gloss Polyethylene
- **Full Color Digital Plates** — Digitally printed on White/Natural Aluminum or Polyethylene
- **PolyCard License Plates** — .024" PolyCard with screen printed imprint
- **Motorcycle Plates** — .035" or .023" Hi-Gloss Polyethylene and PolyCard
- **Raised Letter Frames** — Polystyrene frames with hot stamped imprint in multiple colors
- **Chrome Faced Frames** — Polystyrene frames with chrome/metallic face finish

## Target Audience
Business customers (promotional products distributors, print shops, etc.) looking to order custom license plates and frames in bulk quantities (125–5000+ units).

## Goals
- Present product information clearly with pricing
- Provide downloadable templates for customer artwork preparation
- Maintain up-to-date pricing and product information
- Serve as a lightweight, fast-loading static site
