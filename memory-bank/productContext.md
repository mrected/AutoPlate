# Product Context: AutoPlate.net

## Why This Project Exists
AutoPlate is a manufacturer of custom promotional license plates and frames. The website exists to give business customers a self-service way to:
- Browse available products and their specifications
- See pricing at different quantity tiers
- Download design templates for preparing artwork
- Understand art requirements before submitting orders

## Problems It Solves
- **Product Discovery**: Customers can see all product options, materials, and finishes without needing to call/email sales
- **Pricing Transparency**: Quantity-based pricing tables let customers estimate costs immediately
- **Artwork Preparation**: Templates and art specification pages reduce back-and-forth about artwork requirements
- **Supplier Verification**: Displaying ASI/SAGE/UPIC codes helps distributors verify AutoPlate as an approved supplier

## How It Should Work
1. User lands on homepage with product category cards
2. Navigates to a specific product page via dropdown menu or homepage links
3. Each product page shows:
   - Product image
   - Material/thickness options
   - Pricing table by quantity tier (125, 250, 500, 1000, 2000, 5000)
   - Additional per-color pricing where applicable
4. Art & Templates section provides downloadable PDF templates and art specification guidelines
5. Download Catalogs section links to the latest PDF catalog

## User Experience Goals
- Clean, professional appearance with dark theme and yellow/red accent colors
- Mobile-responsive layout using Bootstrap 3 grid
- Fast loading with minimal dependencies
- Consistent navigation across all pages with dropdown menus
- Clear visual hierarchy: product image → specifications → pricing tables
